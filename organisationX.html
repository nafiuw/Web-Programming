Secondary streams: merchandise sales ( OrganizationX branded gear for community identity). Conditional stream: an optional small membership fee — only if absolutely required, and always waivable.

The pledge at the bottom is non-negotiable: accessibility first. We would rather close the organization than turn away a talented student who cannot pay. Every decision we make is filtered through this principle.
